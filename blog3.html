Top things to see and do in Zanzibar

This is a fantastic and comprehensive guide for anyone planning a trip to Zanzibar in 2026! You've beautifully captured the essence of the island, balancing its historical depth with its natural beauty and adventurous spirit.

🌿 Highlight Experience

To make this excellent list even more helpful for 2026 planning, here are a few minor additions and points of consideration a traveler might find useful:

Enhancements & Pro-Tips for 2026 Travelers:


  • Stone Town Tip: Add that getting "lost" in the labyrinthine streets of Stone Town is part of the charm, but having a basic map or a data plan for your phone is wise.
  • Spice Tour: Emphasize that this is a sensory experience! You often get to taste exotic fruits and spices straight from the tree/plant.
  • Jozani Forest:Mention that the forest boardwalks are a great way to explore the mangrove ecosystem without getting your feet wet.
  • Historical Context: You rightly mention Prison Island and the "Anguish House" (more commonly known as the Slave Market Museum or "Anglican Cathedral"). It's a profoundly moving and important visit to understand Zanzibar's history.
  • Forodhani Gardens: This is a must-do evening activity. Go with an empty stomach and be adventurous—try the Zanzibar pizza (it's not what you think!), sugarcane juice, and urojo (Zanzibari soup).
  • Safari Blue: This is a very popular and well-regarded tour operator, and the name has become synonymous with the day-trip concept itself. It's a great suggestion for a full day of marine fun.

A Note on Timing for 2026:

While 2026 is a great time to visit, it's always smart to check the seasonal weather. The long dry season (June-October) and the short dry season (January-February) generally offer the best weather for beaches and activities. The long rains (March-May) can be quite wet.

Best Time to Visit Zanzibar

  • June – October(Cool, Dry Season):
    Best for: Beach holidays, snorkeling, diving, Stone Town tours, spice farms.

  • December to February (Hot, Dry Season):
    Best for: Festivals (e.g., Sauti za Busara in Feb), island hopping, nightlife.

  • March & November:
    Warm and short rains, but showers are brief and prices are better.
