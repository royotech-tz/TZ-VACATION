About Us

We are passionate travel specialists dedicated to creating authentic, memorable, and seamless tour experiences across Tanzania and beyond.

From thrilling wildlife safaris and iconic mountain treks to relaxing beach escapes, our team combines local expertise with international standards of service. Every journey we design is guided by safety, comfort, and a deep respect for nature and culture.

With carefully selected guides, reliable logistics, and personalized itineraries, we turn travel dreams into unforgettable realities.

Tanzania Vacation is an established tours and travel company based in Arusha,Tanzania. Our specialty is the ability to organize unique tours and safaris to fit every type of tourist both local and international.
